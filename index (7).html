Privacy

Your information stays in its place, too.

Last updated October 7, 2026

Information collected here

If you sign up for shop updates or use the contact form, we collect the information you submit, such as your name, email address, and message. The forms are processed through the website host.

How it is used

If you opt in to shop updates, we may use your name and email address to send product and restock news. We also use submitted information to respond to messages, improve the website, and understand interest in Care Don't Carry Co. We do not sell subscriber information.

Purchases

Purchases take place on Printify. Printify and its payment providers collect the information needed to process payment, produce products, deliver orders, and provide customer support. Their privacy terms apply on those pages.

Leaving the list

You may unsubscribe from shop update emails at any time by replying with “unsubscribe” or contacting us through the website. Your address will be removed from future mailings.

Questions

Use the contact form for privacy questions or requests concerning information submitted through this website.
