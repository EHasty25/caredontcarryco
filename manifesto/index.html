The manifesto

Burnout is not proof that you cared enough.

Caring about your work is not the same as carrying everything. We make reminders for the people who are responsible, committed, and done with letting work become their whole identity.

01

Your title is not your name.

You had a personality before the job description. The role is something you do with your hours, not the ceiling on who you are. When the title changes — and it will — you should still recognize yourself.

02

The problem is not caring.

Caring is the good part. The problem is a culture that treats exhaustion like a credential, and rest like something you have to earn back after proving you didn't need it.

03

Putting work in its place is not quitting.

Boundaries are not the opposite of commitment. The people who last are the ones who learned where the job ends — not the ones who never found the edge.

04

Work can matter without becoming your whole life.

We are not anti-work. We are anti-erasure. Do good work, take it seriously, then go live the rest of it — that part is not a reward, it is the design.

“Caring deeply is not the same as carrying everything.”
